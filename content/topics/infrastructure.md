Most of the resources released in the period are for evaluation. A benchmark of 68 open Erdős problems, fixed in advance and checked in Lean, gave the first controlled measurement of AI systems on open mathematics ({{frontiermath-erdos}}). A test built from senior researchers' corrections of research agents found that current models usually prefer the agent's original plan ({{scipivot-pi-judgment}}), and another benchmark audits AI peer reviewers ({{auditing-ai-peer-reviewers}}). Google DeepMind released the weights of its operational weather model ({{weathernext-2-and-weathernext-cyclones}}).
